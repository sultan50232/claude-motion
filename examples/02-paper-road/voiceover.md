# Voice-over script: The Road of Paper (درب الورق)

One take per line, saved as `public/paper-road/vo/NN.wav`. The slot is how long the caption is on screen; the take should end before the slot does (the cue sheet warns otherwise). If a take needs more room, move `t`/`end` for that line in `src/history/stories/paper-road.json` and everything (captions, gestures, music ducking) follows.

Delivery: warm documentary storyteller, Modern Standard Arabic, measured pace (about 2.5 words per second), a small pause on every «…».
