# 02 · ليش بعض الروائح ترجع لك ذكريات من طفولتك؟

59 s · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 30 fps · stereo, −14 LUFS · Arabic (Saudi dialect) voiceover + subtitles · source: [`sims/scent/`](../../sims/scent/)

The first episode of a TikTok science series: why a smell can bring back a childhood memory. A toy-style 3D young man smells a perfume, a childhood memory island appears, then the science: receptors in the nose → olfactory bulb → amygdala (emotion) and hippocampus (memory), and the caveat that not every smell brings a memory and memories are not exact.

## Prompt

A production brief (in Arabic) asking for a finished, ready-to-post TikTok video, not just a script: a given voiceover text, 50–60 s, 9:16, Saudi dialect, ages 16–35, "educational cinematic motion graphics" in modern 3D with 2D elements, sky blue / calm violet / white, no old cartoon look or loud orange; eight scenes with suggested timings; ElevenLabs for voice; licensed low music, light SFX, synced Arabic subtitles; check the science; export `TikTok_Episode_01.mp4`.

## Process

What the agent did: wrote and timed the voiceover from the brief (small dialect edits, added «البصلة الشمّية»), generated four ElevenLabs takes and picked one by speech-to-text accuracy, rebuilt the scene timing from the real word timestamps (8 scenes, 59.3 s), built five three.js sets procedurally (no image or video generator was available), reviewed stills at every beat over several passes, composed the music bed in code, mixed and mastered to −14 LUFS, and verified the final mix with speech-to-text again.

Details, scene list and the science check: [`sims/scent/README.md`](../../sims/scent/README.md).
