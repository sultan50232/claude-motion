Put one voice-over take per caption line here: `01.wav`, `02.wav`, … in the order of
`lines` in `src/history/stories/paper-road.json`. 16- or 24-bit PCM WAV, any sample rate.
`npm run paper:sfx` places each take at its line's start and ducks the music under it.
The line texts and their time slots are listed in `examples/02-paper-road/voiceover.md`.
